# Contextual vocabulary and transfer practice

## Extraction
Extract difficult and high-value words/phrases from the supplied exam, not a generic frequency list. Group by passage or task. For each item include:
- exact expression and source location;
- part of speech / syntactic pattern where useful;
- meaning in this sentence;
- typical collocation or complement;
- register / connotation / countability / transitivity when relevant;
- a natural example or transferable paraphrase;
- common confusion or misuse.

Separate directly tested items from clearly labelled extensions. Include phrasal verbs, discourse markers, collocations, polysemy and question-stem-to-passage paraphrases.

## D-passage analysis
For each question include answer, exact paragraph/sentence evidence, reasoning chain, paraphrase mapping, each distractor's specific failure and a reusable solving strategy. Also summarize each paragraph's role and the whole-text structure.

## Seven-option passage analysis
For every blank:
- inspect the sentence before and after;
- identify paragraph purpose and rhetorical move;
- track pronouns, demonstratives, repeated concepts, synonyms, connectors, contrast and time sequence;
- explain why the selected sentence fits both locally and globally;
- diagnose the most tempting unused option.

## Extension exercises
Create source-linked vocabulary-in-context, paraphrase, inference, cohesion and writing-beat exercises. Supply answers and explanations. Do not reuse original questions verbatim as the only practice and do not introduce unsupported facts.
