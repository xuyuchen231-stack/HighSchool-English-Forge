# Evidence-based Gaokao benchmark — 2024 New Curriculum National I English

## Public sources inspected
- https://gaokao.eol.cn/e_html/gk/gkst/2024st.shtml (multi-year paper directory)
- https://zy.21cnjy.com/20568421 (answer key and grammar notes)
- https://gsimg.gengsan.com/ziyuan/wenjian/tupian/2024/06/1718157974302055.pdf (paper with commentary; contains demonstrably conflicting key entries)
- https://www.cpsenglish.com/index.php/article/1222 (specialist discussion of disputed grammar)

## Findings and changes
1. Source conflict is a first-class failure mode. The gengsan commentary assigns some answers inconsistent with 21cnjy's key and with its own options. Do not blindly ingest third-party explanations.
2. Reading: 21-35 = CBBAC DADAA CBCCD in 21cnjy; inspect evidence at passage level before asserting accuracy.
3. Seven-option reading: 36-40 = FBEAD per 21cnjy, not the inconsistent sequence in the other source.
4. Cloze: 41-55 = CADCB DCBBA DADBC per 21cnjy; avoid copying the conflicting third-party annotations.
5. Grammar: 56 engineering; 57 functional; 58 to give; 59 closed; 60 walks; 61 the; 62 favourites/favorites; 63 as; 64 that; 65 richness. Q64 has specialist discussion about possible variants: record alternative acceptability only after syntax analysis.
6. Listening: publicly available question-only papers are insufficient for evidence-based explanation. Verify audio or transcript before completion.
7. Writing: detect mismatched salutation/addressee and implausible plot invented by example websites.

## Benchmark coverage limitation
These are examples reviewed, not an exhaustive corpus, no fine-tuning, and not a complete independent blind test.
