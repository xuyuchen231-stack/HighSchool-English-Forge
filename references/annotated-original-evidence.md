# Annotated original paper and deep reading explanation — mandatory

## Source acquisition gate
Obtain the exact complete original examination paper from a user-provided file or a lawfully accessible source, including all passages, options, diagrams, writing prompts and listening materials. Do not reconstruct the paper from answer websites or summaries. If the original is missing, stop before claiming a final integrated examination PDF. Respect rights and distribution restrictions for third-party papers.

## Original-paper gate
The deliverable begins with the intact original pages, unaltered. Follow them with a separately marked *annotated copy* of each reading passage (not a replacement for the original). Maintain original pagination and original question numbering.

## Evidence annotations
For every reading question: (1) identify the precise evidence span in the passage, (2) highlight or underline that span in the annotated copy and add a legible question-number marker at its margin, (3) connect it to the answer via explicit paraphrase, inference or discourse logic, (4) distinguish direct evidence from an inferred conclusion, (5) explain why each distractor fails. For evidence spread across sentences, mark every necessary span. Do not highlight an arbitrary sentence merely to provide an annotation.

## Full-depth student lesson
For each passage include a complete paragraph-by-paragraph Chinese explanation and translation, discourse map, core vocabulary with pronunciation/meaning/collocations, difficult sentence decomposition (subject/predicate/object/modifiers/clauses), and prerequisite micro-lessons with original examples and common misconceptions. For every question include the original stem and all choices where authorized, a justified answer, precise source location, paraphrase mapping, and four-option analysis. Apply equivalent depth to seven-choice gap fill, cloze, grammar fill and writing; listening requires audio or verified transcript.

## Annotation design
Use restrained highlights and margin callouts Q21/Q22 etc. Ensure annotations do not obscure printed text, have sufficient contrast, and are readable at A4 size. A long evidence span may use multiple short underlines. Place the full rationale on the explanation page, not on top of the original text. Render every page and visually inspect annotation alignment, text coverage and cropped elements.

## Acceptance tests
- Complete original included and legible.
- Every reading item has marked evidence in its own annotated passage; unsupported questions explicitly flagged rather than invented.
- Every option explained, with original wording/meaning, elimination rationale and supporting passage logic.
- All passages translated and all complex sentences taught from first principles.
- No final claim while original pages, evidence spans, or listening source are missing.
