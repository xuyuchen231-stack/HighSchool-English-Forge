# Chapter-by-Chapter Exam Workflow

Create an intake manifest for every uploaded paper: filename, upload order, page count, source type, exam title/date, official key, audio/transcript and attachments. Select only the first paper in upload order; keep others queued and untouched.

The first chapter follows printed order. If it is Listening and no usable audio/transcript exists, mark it Blocked and ask whether the user will provide audio or explicitly skip it. Do not silently skip. Do not guess.

For each chapter, complete source inspection, independent solving, answer ledger, full explanations, chapter vocabulary, targeted transfer exercises, independent review, chapter PDF export and page-render QA. Then stop and wait for the next instruction.

Only after all chapters are accepted may the agent assemble the intact original-paper PDF, full answer-and-explanation PDF in original order, chapter vocabulary and extension exercises, plus QA report. Paper 2 remains untouched until Paper 1 is complete and the user authorizes starting it. No cross-paper vocabulary banks or merged analysis during single-paper production.
