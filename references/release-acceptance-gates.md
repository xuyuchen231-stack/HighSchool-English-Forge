# Release Acceptance Gates

## Scope
Exactly one active paper and one chapter per work unit. Question IDs and source pages accounted for. Other papers remain queued.

## Answer accuracy
Independent answer ledger exists; every item has reasoning; all distractors addressed; official-key comparison recorded where available; uncertainty disclosed.

## Teaching quality
Explanations accessible to weak-foundation learners. Vocabulary grounded in active chapter. Extensions labelled. Exercises include answers and explanations.

## PDF
Original paper retained separately and unmodified. Chapter PDF opens and all pages render. No clipping, missing glyphs, unreadable tables, orphan headings or missing diagrams. Complete solution PDF is not labelled complete until every chapter is accepted.

## PPT
English-only. Classroom-appropriate information density. Consistent editorial grid and restrained academic palette. Every slide has a distinct teaching function. Every slide rendered and visually inspected. Source fidelity, English correctness and readability checked.

## Transparency
Disclose missing audio, unreadable text, ambiguous answers and non-official answers. Never describe a draft or partial chapter as a fully audited final product.
